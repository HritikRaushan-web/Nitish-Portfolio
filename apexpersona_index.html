FAQ

Jo aap soch rahe ho.What you may be wondering.

Account kiske naam par banega?Whose name will the account be in?

Aapke hi naam aur documents par. Hum process handle karte hain, account aapka rehta hai.Yours, on your own documents. We handle the process, but the account stays yours.

GST zaroori hai?Is GST required?

Marketplace aur category par depend karta hai. Myntra aur Nykaa mein GST ke saath brand documents bhi lagte hain, aur approval unka decision hota hai. Meesho par kuch cases mein GST ke bina start ho sakta hai. WhatsApp par apna case batao, hum seedha bata denge.It depends on the marketplace and category. Myntra and Nykaa need GST plus brand documents, and approval is their decision. On Meesho some sellers can start without GST. Tell us your case on WhatsApp and we will tell you straight.

Product kitne din mein live hoga?How soon will my product go live?

Yeh marketplace, category aur documents par depend karta hai. Kaam shuru karne se pehle hum WhatsApp par clear timeline bata dete hain.It depends on the marketplace, category and documents. Before we start, we share a clear timeline on WhatsApp.

Budget kam hai. Kya ₹4,999 mein start ho sakta hai?My budget is small. Can I start at ₹4,999?

Haan, Launch Pack ₹4,999 se start hota hai. Aapke products aur marketplaces ke hisaab se scope WhatsApp par tay hota hai. GST alag se lagu hai. No hidden charges.Yes, the Launch Pack starts at ₹4,999. The scope is agreed on WhatsApp based on your products and marketplaces. GST is extra. No hidden charges.
